# Sides and compliance · 三侧、六个开关、三档标签

Condensed rules from the GEO Playbook (general edition), chapters 0, 5.2–5.3 and 8.2. Full text (Chinese): https://canlah.ai/zh/playbook/start/ · https://canlah.ai/zh/playbook/write-pages/#g5-5-2-三侧速查-一-先判身份-价格-促销与赠送-结果数字

**This is not legal advice.** In the general edition, a compliance label describes how firmly a pattern is backed in an *example* industry. It is not the law of the user's industry. Until the user's own rule text has been found, treat every item as "source text not obtained" and write on the stricter side.

## 1. Decide the side first (三问判侧)

Every page, email and listing depends on this. Ask the user; do not guess.

| # | Question | Answer → |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does opening the business, or doing the work, require a licence or a personal professional registration? | **No** → provisionally *unregulated*, then run the three reverse checks below. **Yes** → Q2 |
| 2 | May prices be written as a range? | **No** → **strictly regulated** (stop here). **Yes** → Q3 |
| 3 | Are testimonials or peer comparisons banned outright? | **Banned outright** → strictly regulated. **Allowed with conditions** → **lightly regulated**. **No specific advertising rules** (only general consumer protection) → **unregulated**, even if a licence is needed |

Then, for every side: **are peer comparisons banned** (including unnamed market ranges and ranges quoted from third parties)? Yes → switch E is on.

Reverse checks after a "No" on Q1 (any hit moves the business up one level): the copy makes health, efficacy, earnings, results or employment claims; the audience includes minors; the buyers are abroad.

- **Unsure → go stricter.** Never put "TBD" in the work order. Judging too loose can trigger penalties and joint liability; judging too strict only costs a few tactics and can be undone. 拿不准按更严的一侧开工。
- Example (medical aesthetics): the clinic holds a healthcare licence (Q1 yes); prices must be fixed per tier, no ranges (Q2 no) → strictly regulated; peer comparison is explicitly banned → switch E.
- Full method (regulator documents, product law, reverse checks): https://canlah.ai/zh/playbook/new-industry/#g12-8-2-判侧五步与三条判据

## 2. Six switches (六个开关)

Switches do not change the side; each adds one hard gate on top of it.

| Switch | Hits when | Hard gate |
|---|---|---|
| **A · Agency liability** 代发连带 | Anyone writes or publishes for the business | The agency and the business share liability. Every email or document sent on the client's behalf counts as the client's own. No outreach without a written authorisation (licence holder's signature, licence number, scope, term, how to revoke) and a signed sign-off sheet for each batch of material |
| **B · Legally required fields** 法定必填字段 | The law lists fields an ad must show | Registration numbers and registered names are mandatory; a missing field is a violation. Keep the fields together in a fixed place, repeat them in the fact block, give compulsory disclosures their own page. Use only the legally allowed status word (e.g. "registered"), never upgrade it to "accredited" or "approved" |
| **C · Regulated products** 卖的东西受管 | The company needs no licence, but what it sells must be registered, licensed or notified | Judge the copy per product category, separately from the company |
| **D · Spans two sides** 跨两侧 | One business runs regulated and unregulated lines | The whole site follows the stricter side. Do not split by page or subdomain |
| **E · Peer comparison banned** 禁同行比较 | Comparing with peers is prohibited | No peer price ranges or ranks: named, unnamed, self-calculated or quoted from a third party all count. The structure "market range first, then our price" is itself banned; deleting the words "within the market range" does not fix it |
| **F · Referral commissions banned** 禁佣金引荐 | Paying for referrals is prohibited | No channel priced per lead, per sale or by commission. Only fixed rate-card placements |

Most often missed: A (it applies even if the client never advertises), C (company passes, copy does not), D.

## 3. What forks by side (六步按侧分叉)

Door, identity, target selection and measurement are the same on every side. Only these six steps fork:

| Step | Strictly regulated | Lightly regulated | Unregulated |
|---|---|---|---|
| Question pool | Price questions must be answerable with a fixed final price | No limit | No limit |
| Prices | One fixed final price per item, tax basis stated; no "from / as low as", no ranges (use tiers, each with its defining condition); no price changes, original prices, discounts, packages, time limits or instalments | Ranges and billing method allowed, with scope, exclusions and third-party fees | Ranges and billing method allowed, with scope and exclusions; mandatory fees included in the displayed price |
| Page bans | No laudatory words even if true; no effect, success-rate or recovery-time claims; no before/after images; no testimonials or reviews (screenshots and stars included); no peer comparison, named or not; no "free / complimentary / $0"; no third-party directory that claims government approval; nothing that cannot be proven | Laudatory words must be provable; testimonials with conditions; compare options and delivery formats, not institutions (conservative line) | Provable claims allowed; cases and testimonials allowed; peer comparison pages allowed (same basis, sourced, check date) |
| Video | Only process steps, duration and number of visits, device models, cost structure and inclusions, who should not do it; no effects, no before/after, no clients, no peers | Same bans as that side's pages | No limit |
| Off-site targets | Do not buy or pitch "Best / Top" list spots (disclosing payment does not make it legal); only send corrections to such pages; main targets are official registers, association profiles, manufacturer locators, educational bylined articles | Compete for editorial spots, send update and correction letters; with switch F, no lead- or commission-priced platforms | Best / Top lists are normal targets; before paying for a spot, confirm the page is already being cited |
| Reviews | Never request; passive observation only, never a KPI or acceptance measure | May request: no incentive, no ghost-writing, no filtering, no scripted content; show a single review on your page only under testimonial rules (written consent, name, relationship, year) | May request (same limits); may display as visible text with sample size, period and source; no self-ratings |

## 4. Page types with side limits (有限制的 15 型)

Replacements for the strict side (same intent, legal page type):

- pt03 official pricing page → pt01 single-service price pages
- pt05 list page ("best X") → pt43 buyer's selection framework (criteria, no ranking, no names). Not measured, confidence 65%
- pt37 category list page → the business's own service catalogue
- pt36 case-law page → pt32 regulatory document alignment
- pt24 third-party review, pt28 review aggregate, pt29 self-built reputation page, pt41 verdict-first page, pt42 time-limited promotion → stop
- pt46 market observation → do not schedule (lightly regulated side too)

Seven types no side may self-build (only get listed, fill in your own row, or write a downstream page that quotes them): pt10, pt15, pt20, pt23, pt25, pt32, pt33.

With switch E, rewrite every cell that invites a comparison with peers. The full per-type table (build / adapt / cite-only / stop per side) is in [page-types.md](page-types.md) and https://canlah.ai/zh/playbook/start/#g0-0-3-哪些页型对你有限制-改做哪一型

## 5. Prices, promotions, result numbers (5.2)

**Official benchmark prices.** Strict: only in a separate section with no own price inside it, with table number and update date; putting it in the same table as your price counts as price comparison (explicit in the rule text). Same or adjacent paragraph, or "we are within / below the benchmark": conservative line, default no. Lightly regulated: copy official fees verbatim with table name and retrieval date; if the original table cannot be obtained, write no number, only "charged per the official fee schedule, see <link>". Never estimate, never reuse an old value.

**Subsidised prices.** Strict: never merge a subsidy into a price ("$X after subsidy"); subsidy amounts appear only in the official section. Other sides: list the post-subsidy amount separately with eligibility, official URL and check date.

**Peer price ranges.** Strict and switch E: never. Lightly regulated without E: only as a sourced standalone fact section (sample, source, date), never in the same table, adjacent paragraph or FAQ answer as your price (conservative line). Unregulated: may share a table.

**What fills the gap.** Strict: the official benchmark section plus variable sentences without peer prices. Switch E: an *official-basis sentence* (what the law, the regulator or the procedure says, in the same slot, length and pattern as a market sentence, 40–60 words) plus a *procedure-variable sentence* (the subject is the procedure, not peers).

**Feeds.** Market sentences and ranges never go into product feeds or structured data. Feeds carry only your actual prices (all sides).

**Title price hooks.** Unregulated: "From $X" + unit or condition, or three tier prices; no unsourced "cheapest". Strict: fixed final price incl. tax + unit + brand; never From, ranges, discounts or subsidised prices. Lightly regulated: "From $X" only if the defining condition and the cost drivers follow immediately.

**Promotions.** Strict: no time-limited offers, original prices, discounts, "free" or "complimentary" (explicit); a bundle becomes one standing fixed price on its own URL; rework and replacement policies are written as period, number of times and what is already included. With switch B, any promotion shown must carry its full legal terms in the same block.

**Result numbers** (success rates, grades, case counts, patient counts, ratings) must pass six checks before they go on a page: definition, denominator, period, sample selection, calculation method, permission to publish. Strict side adds four gates, all required: no adjectives or comparatives; a ledger export signed by the business owner; never in the same sentence as effects, suitability or outcomes; no price-change percentages. Case and procedure counts are deleted by default on the strict side. Write every result number as a self-contained sentence: "M of N, i.e. X%, in <period>".

## 6. Reviews, comparisons, lists, titles, links (5.3)

- **Who controls a review decides whether it is your ad**: if you invited, filtered or rewarded it, or copied it to your site, it is yours. Your reply is your content too.
- **Replies (all sides)**: never confirm or deny that the reviewer is a client; no service names, prices, effects or peer comparisons; one line of thanks or "please contact us at <phone>"; reply within 72 hours, negative reviews with one template.
- **Comparisons**: strict compares options and methods, never institutions, never "better / more advanced"; switch E allows no peer column or market-range column; lightly regulated needs written permission to use another firm's name or logo.
- **Lists**: strict never quotes third-party ranks, ratings or reviews on its own pages; with switch E a third-party rank may be stated for the business itself, without implying superiority ("ranked above X", "only one listed" are comparisons).
- **Titles and credentials**: strict never writes Leading / Expert / best / senior / experienced; no specialist claim without specialist registration; only regulator-approved qualifications. Lightly regulated: "Expert" only with documented evidence.
- **Outbound links**: strict links only to government and regulator pages (not vendors, not locators); being listed on a manufacturer's locator is an inbound link and is fine. Links and bylines are not citation conditions on any side.
- **FAQ, captions, table headers, title and meta description are not exempt.** A draft that only deletes and does not refill is returned for low density; record "removed X, replaced with Y".

## 7. Every ban has a replacement (禁令都配替代写法)

| Banned on the strict side | Write this in the same slot |
|---|---|
| Peer price ranges, market sentences | Official benchmark in its own section + procedure-variable sentence |
| "From / as low as / original price / discount" | Tiered fixed final prices, each tier with its defining condition |
| "Free replacement / free check-up" | "Included in the quoted price, once" |
| Subsidised price in the same table | Subsidy in its own section with table number and update date |
| Success rates, rescue rates | Process duration, risks and suitability conditions |
| "Most cases", "highly experienced" | Adjective-free operating facts backed by a ledger (e.g. practising since 2011, registration numbers on the team page) |

"What we do not do" sentences are among the most valuable: they cannot be ad copy, and AI quotes them readily.

## 8. Three compliance labels and sign-off (三档标签与签字)

Every compliance sentence carries exactly one label, taken from the source text:

| Label | Writer's handling |
|---|---|
| **Explicit in the rule text** 条文明文 | Follow it. No exception, no sign-off can relax it |
| **Conservative line, not explicit in the rule text** 保守线，非条文明文 | Follow by default; relax only if the client's compliance lead gives written reasons, archived |
| **Source text not obtained** 未取到原文 | Signing does not release it. Write the default wording, treat it as neither allowed nor banned, never estimate official numbers; use a pointer sentence ("per the official schedule, see <link>") until the text is found |

Where labels come from: a label already in the source is copied verbatim; a sentence with only a clause number is "explicit" (the number must match the rule text in the industry edition); an inference or internal policy is "conservative line"; nothing marked → escalate to the editor. **A writer never adds "explicit" on their own.** Any ban without a quoted rule sentence behind it is "conservative line".

**Stop writing and ask the client's compliance lead** in three situations: (1) facts only the client knows (did they buy review or ranking packages, is the evidence of specialism on file, where is the certifying body registered, is this the final tax-inclusive price); (2) items labelled conservative line; (3) items labelled source text not obtained. **A signature can relax only (1) and (2).** It never turns something explicitly banned into something publishable, and it does not remove the agency's joint liability under switch A.

**Five more stop conditions before writing anything:** the advertiser's identity is unclear; the price cannot be fixed as a final price; a number fails the six checks; the client has not authorised it; the comparison bases differ. Hand it to the compliance lead. Never fill in a number yourself.

## 9. What the agent writes down (产出)

First line of the work order, before any other task. It is the same line as in SKILL.md step 0; the compliance fields are:

```
Side: <strict | light | unregulated> · Switches: <A,B,C,D,E,F or none> · Unsure items (treated as stricter): <list> · Compliance lead: <name or none> · Written authorisation (switch A): <on file | missing | n/a> · …
```

The rest of the line (scope, acceptance tier, top 3 buyer types, languages, blockers) is specified in SKILL.md step 0 and https://canlah.ai/zh/playbook/templates/#g14-B-4-工单-台账与月报表头

If switch A is on and the authorisation is missing: stop. There is no downgrade path for that refusal.
